# Deep job-fit analysis

## Tools
`get_market_overview`, multiple focused `search_jobs`, `get_job_details`, `get_job_keywords`

## Workflow
Inventory known background → expand to adjacent titles → live search → score fit/stretch → deepen top 3–5.

## Guardrails
Every recommended opening from tools only. No fake job ids.
