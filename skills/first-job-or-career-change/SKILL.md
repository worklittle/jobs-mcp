# First job or career change

## Workflow
1. Note transferable life skills (customer service, caregiving, driving, bilingual, retail).
2. Search entry titles; omit `seniority_level` unless they asked for entry/new_grad explicitly as a filter.
3. Prefer trainers-welcome / will-train language when details confirm.

## Output
Starter shortlist + what to say in a simple application.
