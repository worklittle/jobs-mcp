# Jobs that pay more than you think

Find **normal jobs** with better-than-expected pay.

## Tools
- `get_market_overview` for salary signals by role/geo
- `search_jobs` with `salary_min` once you know a floor
- `get_job_details` to verify pay isn’t bait

## Workflow
1. Pick everyday categories: trades helpers, CDL, dental assisting, ultrasound, HVAC helper, pharmacy tech, underwriting associate, claims, union-adjacent, night differential healthcare, etc. Match to user location/skills.
2. Use market overview + listings with known salaries.
3. Call out when pay needs certs/shift work.

## Output
List “sleeper” roles with pay evidence from tools only.
