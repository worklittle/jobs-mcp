# No degree needed

## Workflow
Search titles that rarely demand degrees: trades, logistics, sales floor, support, ops, care, food service management track, apprenticeships.
When details mention “bachelor’s required,” demote or skip.

## Tools
`search_jobs`, `get_job_details` to verify education lines.
