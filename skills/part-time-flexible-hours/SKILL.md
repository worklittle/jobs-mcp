# Part-time or flexible hours

## Tools
`search_jobs` with `employment_type: part_time` when relevant; also try query phrases like evening, weekend, flexible for industries that encode hours in titles.

## Output
Schedule-friendly picks; note when flexibility isn’t confirmed in the posting.
